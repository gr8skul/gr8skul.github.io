Create. Publish. Maintain. Leave.

You made something. You want to share it. You don't need a dashboard telling you what it's worth.

GR8SKUL Publisher is a deliberately simple Windows application for uploading and managing YouTube videos without opening YouTube Studio.

  1. CREATEMake your work on your own terms.
  2. PUBLISHChoose a video, give it a title and description, and send it to YouTube.
  3. MAINTAINReview your video library and update titles or descriptions when needed.
  4. LEAVEClose the program. Get back to creating.

NOT AN ANALYTICS DASHBOARD

No view counts, optimization scores, growth prompts, or performance comparisons. Publisher doesn't prevent you from visiting YouTube Studio later; it simply keeps those metrics out of the publishing workflow.

YOUR COMPUTER. YOUR YOUTUBE ACCOUNT.

The Windows application connects directly to Google's YouTube services. Video uploads and YouTube authorization do not pass through a GR8SKUL-operated intermediary server.

ONE PURCHASE. NO SUBSCRIPTION.

GR8SKUL Publisher is planned as a one-time purchase. Public sales and downloads will be available after release preparations are complete.
