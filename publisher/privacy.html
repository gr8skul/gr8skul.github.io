PRIVACY POLICY

GR8SKUL Publisher · Updated November 21, 2026

GR8SKUL Publisher is a Windows desktop application for uploading videos to YouTube and managing videos on a YouTube channel you authorize. This policy describes the Google user data the application accesses, why it is needed, where it is stored, and how to remove access and local records. This policy applies to GR8SKUL Publisher, not to the separate GR8SKUL music-listening service.

1. GOOGLE ACCOUNT AUTHORIZATION AND PERMISSIONS

You connect your YouTube account through Google's authorization screen. GR8SKUL Publisher does not request or store your Google account password. The application requests these OAuth permissions:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos you select to your authorized YouTube channel.
  • youtube.readonly — retrieve your channel's uploaded-video library and associated information so you can view it in the application.
  • youtube.force-ssl — read and manage authorized YouTube resources, including editing video metadata, changing supported video settings, setting thumbnails, and deleting videos when you explicitly request those actions.

These permissions are requested to support the corresponding user-facing features. You can decline authorization; YouTube-connected features will then be unavailable.

2. GOOGLE USER DATA ACCESSED

Depending on the feature you use, the application accesses your authorization tokens and YouTube channel or video information, including channel identifiers and upload-playlist information; video identifiers, titles, descriptions, tags, categories, thumbnails, publication dates, durations, visibility and other supported video settings. It may retrieve current video information before applying an edit or deletion.

The application does not separately collect or create aggregated or anonymized Google account data for advertising, profiling, or analytics. Its local transfer history is used to display the status of your own publishing activity.

3. HOW INFORMATION IS USED

Google user data is used only to provide and maintain the YouTube features you request: connecting your account, displaying your library, uploading selected video files, updating video information or thumbnails, and deleting selected videos after confirmation. The application uses authorization tokens to make those requests to YouTube API Services on your behalf.

When you publish a video, the selected video file and its metadata are transferred from your computer to YouTube. GR8SKUL does not operate an intermediary server that normally receives those video files or Google authorization tokens for the desktop Publisher workflow.

4. LOCAL STORAGE AND SECURITY

GR8SKUL Publisher stores its Google authorization token, upload history, and saved application preferences in the current Windows user's local application-data area, under GR8SKUL/Publisher Windows Test. Temporary video files may be created on your computer while uploads are processed and are removed after the upload attempt finishes. The application communicates with its own local interface through a server bound to 127.0.0.1, rather than exposing that interface as a public internet service.

The Publisher uses local request-control checks for sensitive actions. Google account credentials are not intentionally transmitted to GR8SKUL's website as part of normal publishing. Locally saved tokens are not currently protected by a separate application-level encryption system; the security of those files depends in part on your Windows account, device permissions, and device security. Do not share your Windows profile or application data with untrusted people.

5. SHARING, TRANSFERS, AND LIMITED USE

The application sends the information needed for your requested actions to Google's YouTube API Services. GR8SKUL does not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use it for targeted advertising. GR8SKUL does not transfer Google user data to unrelated third parties for their independent marketing purposes. Information you voluntarily include in a support request may be used to respond to that request; please do not send authorization tokens or other secrets.

GR8SKUL Publisher uses Google user data only to provide or improve the user-facing features described here, consistent with applicable Google API Services User Data Policy and Limited Use requirements. It does not use Google user data to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

6. RETENTION, DISCONNECTION, AND DELETION

Google authorization tokens remain on your device until you disconnect the YouTube account in Publisher, remove the local token file, or otherwise remove the application's data. Disconnecting YouTube deletes the Publisher's locally saved YouTube authorization token; it does not delete videos already published to YouTube, saved upload preferences, or local transfer history.

You can also revoke the application's authorization in Google Account connections. Revoking access at Google prevents further authorized access but does not automatically remove data already saved on your computer.

You can remove completed, failed, or interrupted transfer-history entries using the Publisher's history-clearing control. To remove all locally retained Publisher information, close the application and delete its local data folder: %LOCALAPPDATA%\GR8SKUL\Publisher Windows Test. This removes locally saved tokens, upload history, and preferences. Deleting this folder does not delete videos from YouTube. If you need help locating or removing local data, contact contact@gr8skul.com.

Videos and their metadata stored by YouTube are retained and deleted under Google's policies and your YouTube account controls. When you deliberately delete a video through the Publisher's Library, the application requests deletion from YouTube; this is separate from clearing local application history.

7. PURCHASE AND SUPPORT INFORMATION

If GR8SKUL Publisher is offered for sale, checkout and payment processing will be described here when those services are launched. Payment providers may process purchase information under their own privacy policies. Any contact information you provide directly to GR8SKUL for customer support is used to respond to your inquiry and is separate from the application's normal YouTube API workflow.

8. YOUTUBE API SERVICES AND YOUR RIGHTS

GR8SKUL Publisher uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features, you also agree to YouTube's Terms of Service. Google's handling of information is described in Google's Privacy Policy. You can review and revoke the application's Google account access at any time using Google's third-party connections page.

9. CHANGES AND CONTACT

We may revise this policy as the Publisher's features, security practices, or distribution change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised date. For privacy questions or data-removal assistance, email contact@gr8skul.com.

This policy describes the current desktop Publisher implementation. It does not mean that Google has approved or verified the application; Google makes that determination separately.
