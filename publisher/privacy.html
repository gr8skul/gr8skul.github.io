Privacy Policy

GR8SKUL Publisher · Effective November 15, 2026

GR8SKUL Publisher is a Windows desktop application that lets you upload videos to YouTube, view your video library, and edit supported video information without using YouTube Studio.

1. GOOGLE ACCOUNT ACCESS

When you connect a YouTube account, Google handles authorization and asks you to grant the permissions the application needs. The application requests YouTube upload, read-only access, and YouTube account management access (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, and youtube.force-ssl). These permissions support uploading videos, listing your videos, and editing video metadata.

GR8SKUL Publisher does not ask for or store your Google account password.

2. HOW YOUR INFORMATION IS USED

The application uses Google authorization tokens to communicate directly with the YouTube API on your behalf. Video files are sent from your computer to YouTube. Video titles, descriptions, identifiers, and related account data may be retrieved or transmitted as needed for the functions you choose.

GR8SKUL does not operate a server that receives your uploaded video files or your Google authorization tokens as part of this desktop application's normal YouTube workflow.

3. LOCAL STORAGE

The application stores information needed for its operation on your computer. This may include Google authorization tokens, local video-library information, application settings, and transfer history. Anyone with access to your computer or its user profile may be able to access locally stored data, depending on your system's security.

4. PURCHASE INFORMATION

When sales begin, purchases are expected to be processed by Payhip and its payment providers, rather than through the desktop application's YouTube connection. Those providers handle checkout and purchase records under their own privacy policies. GR8SKUL may receive the purchase and contact information needed to fulfill an order and provide support. This section will be confirmed against the live checkout before sales launch.

5. SHARING AND ADVERTISING

GR8SKUL Publisher does not sell your YouTube account data or use it to deliver targeted advertising. Google receives data necessary to provide the YouTube service when you use the application. GR8SKUL may respond to lawful requests where required.

6. DISCONNECTING AND REVOKING ACCESS

You can disconnect your account in the application. You can also revoke the application's access through your Google Account connections settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized requests using that connection. Local application records may remain on your computer until removed.

7. GOOGLE AND YOUTUBE

Your use of YouTube remains subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. GR8SKUL Publisher uses YouTube API Services.

8. CHANGES AND CONTACT

We may update this policy as the application or its distribution changes. The current version will be posted here. For privacy questions, contact contact@gr8skul.com.
